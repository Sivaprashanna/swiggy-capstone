# 📖 Data Story – Swiggy Cuisine Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes Swiggy order data to understand revenue and order performance across different cuisine categories. The analysis uses Excel, SQL, and Tableau to transform raw order data into meaningful business insights. The dataset contains **358 orders across 6 cuisines**, generating a total revenue of **₹497,106**, with an average revenue of approximately **₹1,408.50 per order**.

## Key Findings

The analysis shows that revenue performance varies across the different cuisine categories. By comparing revenue and order volumes across cuisines, the dashboard helps identify which cuisine categories contribute more strongly to overall business performance.

The overall revenue generated is **₹497,106**, while the total number of orders is **358**. These KPIs provide a high-level view of the business performance and serve as a baseline for comparing individual cuisine categories.

The Tableau dashboard provides an interactive way to explore these results. Users can examine cuisine-level revenue and order performance and compare the results with the **revenue target**.

## Business Insight

The analysis can help Swiggy understand which cuisine categories are contributing most to revenue and which categories may require additional attention. Comparing actual revenue with the target can also help identify whether the business is achieving its expected performance.

Rather than looking only at the total number of orders, combining **revenue, order volume, and target performance** provides a more complete picture of cuisine performance.

## Conclusion

Overall, the Swiggy Cuisine Analysis demonstrates how raw order data can be transformed into actionable business insights using Excel, SQL, and Tableau. With **₹497,106 in total revenue from 358 orders across 6 cuisines**, the dashboard provides a clear foundation for evaluating cuisine performance and supporting data-driven decisions.

The interactive Tableau dashboard allows users to explore the results visually and identify areas that could contribute to improving revenue performance.
